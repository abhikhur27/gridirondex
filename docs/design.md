# GridironDex: paper & ink

A published playbook that moves. Fans choose a concept, follow its lines and understand a defender's conflict. Redesign of the existing Vite/React app; all 80 lessons, film references, stored bookmarks and share URLs are preserved.

Direction: editorial split, led by one large illustrated field. The persistent sidebar is replaced by an accessible library drawer. A quiet header names the destinations; explanatory notes sit alongside the diagram. Playback and field controls float on rounded white paper, with restrained shadows. Related concepts are a reading list, not dashboard tiles.

Locked palette: warm paper `#F9F8F6`, cardstock `#FFFFFF`, ink `#242B32`, pencil `#68727B`, rule `#E2DFD7`, blue `#0055FF`, goldenrod `#E68A00`, scarlet `#D32F2F`, plum `#7B1FA2`. All CSS colors derive from tokens. Zones use a light plum tint. No glow, dark panels or gradients.

Typography: Newsreader roman 400/500/600 for headlines, field numbers and playbook notes; DM Sans 400/500/600 for navigation, controls and readable explanations. The title is large but quiet. Utilities use sentence case rather than tracked telemetry. Space follows a 4px scale; field, drawer and film surfaces have restrained 16–24px corners; controls alone use a pill silhouette.

Signature: a blue-and-goldenrod route diagram that responds like an annotated printed plate. Hover/focus reveals one assignment and dims unrelated routes and players. Selecting a route or player redraws that assignment with a 900ms eased stroke; repeat selection replays it. The full-play clock draws lines and moves players continuously; scrubbing stays direct. No ambient breathing or decorative motion. Reduced motion shows focused paths immediately.
